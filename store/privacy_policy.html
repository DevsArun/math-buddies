Math Buddies — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Math Buddies is a paid educational game for young children. We designed it to be completely safe for kids:

  • No personal data collected. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
  • No internet. The app works fully offline and never connects to the internet.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. There is no advertising or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • No accounts. No sign-up or login is required.
  • On-device progress only. Stars and stickers are saved locally on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • No in-app purchases. You buy the app once; everything is included forever.

Children's privacy (COPPA): Because the app collects nothing and never goes online, it is fully compliant with children's privacy requirements.

Contact: If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer via the Amazon Appstore developer contact link.
